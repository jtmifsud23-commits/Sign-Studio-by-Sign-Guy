How many coasters are included?
Each $30.00 CAD pack includes four matching custom coasters.

Can I use more than one colour?
Yes. Your design can use up to eight colours in total, including the background, logo and text. All four coasters in a pack share the same design.

What size are the coasters?
Each round coaster is 101.66 mm across and 4 mm thick, with artwork and text flush with the top surface.
